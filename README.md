# Retail-Customer-Insights-Dashboard
Customer shopping behavior analysis using Python, SQL and Power BI to identify purchasing patterns, customer preferences and business insights.

## Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior to understand purchasing patterns, customer preferences, product performance, subscription behavior, discounts, payment methods, and sales trends.

The project uses Python for data cleaning and exploratory data analysis, SQL for business queries, and Power BI for interactive data visualization.
## Dataset

The dataset contains 3,900 customer shopping records with information such as:

- Customer ID
- Age
- Gender
- Item Purchased
- Category
- Purchase Amount
- Location
- Season
- Review Rating
- Subscription Status
- Discount Applied
- Payment Method
- Previous Purchases
- Purchase Frequency

## Key Insights

- The dataset contains 3,900 customers.
- Total sales were approximately $233,081.
- The average purchase amount was approximately $59.76.
- Clothing generated the highest total sales of $104,264.
- Male customers represented approximately 68% of the dataset.
- Only about 27% of customers were subscribed.
- Fall generated the highest seasonal sales.
- Blouse was the highest-selling product by total sales.
- The average customer review rating was approximately 3.75 out of 5.

  ## Business Recommendations

- Maintain strong inventory levels for high-performing Clothing products.
- Increase marketing efforts to convert non-subscribers into subscribers.
- Introduce targeted promotions for weaker categories such as Outerwear.
- Develop loyalty offers for repeat customers.
- Use customer feedback to improve product satisfaction and ratings.
